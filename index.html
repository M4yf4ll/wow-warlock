Recommended leveling build

Affliction first

Current WoW Forever guides recommend Affliction for efficient leveling: instant DoTs, drain-based sustain, and strong multi-target pressure. This is not the old Era build; Forever changes talents such as Suppression, Improved Corruption, Pandemic, and Wrack.

Choose your demon

Voidwalker: safest solo pulls; let it establish threat. Succubus/Incubus: damage and Humanoid control. Felhunter: dispel and interrupt. Imp: ranged damage and Blood Pact for groups.

Undead racial package

Touch of the Grave adds passive damage and healing. Cannibalize restores health and, in Forever, mana from a nearby eligible Humanoid or Undead corpse. Will of the Forsaken is a situational fear/charm/sleep break, not a damage button.

Gear · professions · inventory

Gear for less downtime

A wand is the first major quality-of-life upgrade: it finishes targets without draining mana. For Affliction, prioritize Shadow Spell Power and general Spell Power, then useful sustain and survivability.

Stat priorities

  • Spell Power / Shadow damage for DoTs, drains, and Shadow Bolt.
  • Spell hit is useful, but 4/5 Suppression already supplies 4% by level 13.
  • Critical Strike improves as Pandemic and Malevolence enter the build.
  • Spirit improves Life Tap returns; Intellect and Stamina smooth mana and health pools.

Upgrade checklist

  • Buy or craft the best affordable wand early; keep upgrading it.
  • Compare actual spell stats, not armor rating or item level alone.
  • Keep a Healthstone, food, water, bandages, and bag space for Soul Shards.
  • Tailoring plus Enchanting is a strong self-sufficiency pair; current guides call out crafted class gear and early wands.

Exact best leveling drops are still incomplete in pre-launch references, especially beyond level 35. Treat named loot lists as suggestions, not guaranteed Forever drops; the level-appropriate quest reward or crafted upgrade is usually the practical choice.

What to prioritize

First secure a usable wand, then take the cheap Shadow tailoring upgrades as their level gates arrive. After that, choose gear with Spell Power or Shadow damage, followed by Stamina, Spirit, and Intellect. Keep a quest or dungeon reward if it beats a costly craft.

Profession routine

Loot humanoid cloth while questing. Keep Tailoring near the recipes you want, disenchant spare crafted pieces to feed Enchanting, and make bags if you need more space. Tradeable wands can also be bought, so you don’t have to power-level Enchanting before using one.

The item path comes from pre-launch WoW Forever guides and may change at launch. Check the in-game tooltip and current database before investing gold; item availability, recipe skill, and level requirements can be tuned.

Solo questing · multi-target · dungeons

Rotation by situation

Early levels, before the wand

Keep Demon Skin up. Open with Immolate; add Corruption once learned, then use a modest Shadow Bolt or wand/auto attack to finish. Shadow Bolt is expensive early, so avoid repeatedly hard-casting if the target is already dying.

One durable target

Send the pet first. Apply Immolate if it will tick, then Corruption and Bane of Agony. Add Siphon Life from 30 only if the mob will live long enough. Wand while DoTs tick; use Drain Life when health is low and instant Shadow Bolt on Nightfall procs.

Multiple quest mobs

Let the Voidwalker build threat, then spread Corruption and Bane of Agony across enemies that will survive long enough. Keep moving between instant DoTs. Drain Life to recover; use Nightfall procs on the healthiest target.

Resource loop & boss fights

Use Life Tap when you have safe health headroom, then recover with Drain Life, Cannibalize, food, or bandages. Drain Soul when you need shards. On elites and bosses, maintain long DoTs, use an appropriate utility Curse alongside Bane of Agony, and channel Wrack after your Shadow DoTs are active.

At level 41 and beyond

For solo farming, current guidance favors sacrificing the Felhunter for its health regeneration. If an encounter needs an interrupt, dispel, crowd control, or pet tank, summon the useful demon instead; that removes the sacrifice buff until reapplied.

Dungeon etiquette

Turn off Voidwalker Torment when a tank is holding threat. Keep pet attacks controlled, use Spell Lock on dangerous casts, and bring Healthstones and Soul Shards. Use Rain of Fire on grouped packs; Hellfire is close-range and costs your own health.
